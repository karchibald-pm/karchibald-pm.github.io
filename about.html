About

I start where every AI product really starts: the problem and the data.

I'm an AI product manager with 20+ years in product and business analysis at a national health insurer, a leading cancer center and a global pharma company. My work runs from enterprise data platforms to AI digital assistants and generative AI search.

My strength is the part before the model: finding the real problem, identifying the gaps, mapping where humans must decide, and building the governance that lets AI ship safely. I'm most at home turning messy, regulated problems into products people trust.

I look for ways to align my team’s growth goals with the work ahead. When a business analyst on my team expressed interest in project management, I paired her with a new initiative that needed a project lead, and coached her as a certified PMP.
