How I work

Six principles, each earned on a real project.

  • Start with the why.

    Unpack the real problem and map the current state before deciding what AI should do.

  • AI is only as good as its sources.

    Garbage in, garbage out: understanding the data sources and content quality is critical to trustworthy results and a quality product.

  • Design for trust.

    Every AI answer cites a source; every score comes with an explanation.

  • Know where humans decide.

    Mark which steps need judgment before choosing what AI takes over.

  • Scope tight, roadmap the rest.

    A focused MVP ships; a roadmap keeps the good ideas from getting lost.

  • Raise risks early, and often if needed.

    Keep a risk and decision log so every risk stays visible, and advocate or escalate where appropriate.
