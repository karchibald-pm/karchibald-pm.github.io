Designing an AI Digital Assistant for Clinical Trials

A lifelike AI digital assistant that talks users through clinical trial options on mobile and desktop. Designed to help users find and understand the trials available to them, and take the next step toward enrolling.

Overview

Clinical trials need participants to succeed, but patients and doctors often struggle to find and understand the trials available to them, which slows enrollment. The program set out to change that with a lifelike AI digital assistant that could answer questions about clinical trials and improve clinical trial engagement.

AI hypothesis

We believed

If users can talk with a lifelike AI assistant that pulls back clinical trial information related to their questions, they'll find and understand trials more easily and ultimately feel confident taking the next step toward enrolling.

What I learned

System requirements tell you what a product must do; the user journey tells you what it must get right.

When research shifts your target user, your requirements change with it. For us, the pivot to a new persona meant reimagining the product, which took time to evaluate what was changing and realign. Treating it as a new product earned the time to do it well.

For consumer-facing AI, legal is a design partner, not a final checkpoint. Bringing legal in early shapes what the AI can say, how data is handled and where the guardrails sit, before anything is built.
