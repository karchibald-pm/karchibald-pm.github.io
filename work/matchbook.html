MatchBook: An Explainable AI Marketplace

An AI matchmaker connecting retiring business owners with buyers, where AI explains every match but never does the math. Taken from customer research to a working MVP in an 11-week program.

Working MVP · Demo Day, October 2026

Program
PM Accelerator, 17-person cross-functional team
My role
AI Product Manager leading the Product & Design workstream, alongside two other AI PM–led workstreams and the MatchBook founder; led 2 designers
Timeline
11-week program, August to October 2026 (our team formed in week 3); Demo Day October 6, 2026
Users
Retiring small-business owners (sellers) and acquisition-minded buyers
Product
Two-sided marketplace with explainable AI matching

Overview

About 12 million U.S. businesses are owned by baby boomers nearing retirement, and more than 70% of owners have no succession plan (industry estimates). Sellers can't find buyers, buyers face stale listings they can't verify, and the best businesses never get listed because owners fear exposure. As our team put it: the silver tsunami has no matchmaker.

AI hypothesis

We believed

If buyers and sellers get matches scored by transparent rules, with AI explaining every match in plain language and drafting labeled introductions, they'll trust the marketplace enough to share financials and start a conversation.

How it works: AI input to output

MVP delivered

CapabilityWhat it does
Explainable match scoreScores each buyer–seller match from 0 to 100 across nine categories, using transparent rules.
AI-drafted introductionsThe LLM drafts a warm introduction grounded in the match-score logic, always labeled as AI-written.
AI listing summariesSummarizes each listing so buyers can scan opportunities quickly.
Confidentiality controlsSeller identity stays hidden until the seller approves an introduction; financials sit behind an NDA.

Phase 2 roadmap

CapabilityWhat it will do
Financial verificationVerifies seller financials, the trust signal every buyer surveyed said they need.
Financial dashboardShows a business's key financials, SWOT analysis and overall business fitness in one view.
AI chatbotAnswers questions about listings and guides users through onboarding.
LLM document extractionPulls key details from seller documents, with guardrails and hallucination prevention.

What I learned

Be transparent about what an MVP can and can't do. Clear labels and an honest roadmap keep users' trust while the full product is still being built.

When time runs short, protect the core value proposition. For MatchBook, that was trust.
