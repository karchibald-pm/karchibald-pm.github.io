GenAI Search for 83,000 Employees

Three AI features on a global employee portal: AI-generated search answers, conversational requests in the virtual agent, and chat summaries for support agents. Built to help 83,000 colleagues in 13 countries find answers on their own, before opening a ticket.

Overview

Help tickets were growing, and a review of ticket volume and type showed a clear pattern: many came from colleagues who couldn't find information that already existed in the knowledge base. Leadership decided to enhance the portal with AI so colleagues could find answers on their own. As Business Owner and Product Manager, I partnered with the Digital Product Manager to choose ready-made Now Assist capabilities that fit our colleagues' needs, rather than building custom ones.

AI hypothesis

We believed

If colleagues can ask in plain language and get answers grounded in approved, current articles, with the sources shown and a direct path to act (the right form, site or request), they'll get what they need done on their own, open fewer avoidable tickets, and trust what the AI tells them.

What I learned

AI search is only as good as how its content is structured. The AI could only answer what the knowledge base said, and only as well as the content was organized, so content readiness had to be a launch requirement, not a cleanup project for later.
