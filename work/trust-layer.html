The Trust Layer: Governed Data for Enterprise AI

A governed, self-service document-extraction tool with approvals, access control and data lineage built in. Built to give business areas direct access to the documents they need, and a trusted foundation for AI-powered search.

Overview

Business areas that maintain regulated documents couldn't access the related documents directly. Every request went through a manual process, with documents pulled by hand based on each user's permissions, and a single request could take up to three months. The business also wanted AI-powered search, which would only be as trustworthy as the documents feeding it. We set out to build a governed, self-service way in.

AI hypothesis

We believed

If business areas can request documents themselves through one governed path, with approvals, access control and traceable lineage built in, requests no longer wait months on a manual process, and AI-powered search can be built on content people trust.

What I learned

Enterprise AI is only as trustworthy as the data pipeline under it. Access, approvals and lineage are the unglamorous half of every AI product, and they decide whether AI can scale. And on a lean team, a tight scope and a solid requirements baseline keep the work moving, even when the team changes.
